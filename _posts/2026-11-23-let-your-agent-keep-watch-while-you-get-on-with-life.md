**Let Your Agent Keep Watch While You Get On With Life**

A chatbot answers when you ask. An agent keeps working after you stop talking.

One of the handiest things an agent can do is watch something for you — and tap you on the shoulder only when it matters.

Think of a hawker stall you visit on Saturdays, or a clinic's opening hours before a visit, or the weather for a family outing next weekend. Instead of checking again and again yourself, ask your assistant to do the checking.

Try it today:

1. Pick one thing that changes — for example, "Will it rain on Saturday?"
2. Tell your assistant: "Check the weather for Saturday every morning, and tell me only if rain is expected."
3. Ask it to remind you at a time you choose, such as 8 in the morning.
4. Go about your day. If nothing changes, it stays quiet. If rain appears, it tells you.

That is the agent difference in plain words: a chatbot answers; an agent carries a job — watching, checking, and reporting back, so you only act when it matters.

Try it with something you already check often, and tell us how it went. What would you like your agent to watch for you? Your ideas shape the next sharing.
