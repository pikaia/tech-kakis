**Ask Your AI to Explain a Confusing Letter in Plain Words**

We all get letters that are hard to read — a bank notice, an insurance letter, a government form. The words are long, the sentences are longer, and the important part is buried somewhere in the middle.

Your AI assistant can translate it into plain words for you. It will tell you what the letter is really saying, and what, if anything, you need to do about it.

Try it yourself:

1. Take a photo of the letter, or type out the confusing part.
2. Ask your assistant: "Please explain this letter in simple, plain words. What does it mean, and do I need to do anything?"
3. Then ask a follow-up: "What is the most important thing in this letter?"
4. If anything is still unclear, ask again: "Please explain that part like you would to a friend."

A small warning: never share your full account number, password, or Singpass details with any AI assistant. If the letter asks for those, that is a red flag — check with the bank or office directly instead.
