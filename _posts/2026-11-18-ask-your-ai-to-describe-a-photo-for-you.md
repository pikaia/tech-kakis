**Ask Your AI to Describe a Photo for You**

Sometimes a photo arrives in WhatsApp and the writing in it is too small to read, or you are not quite sure what it shows. Your AI assistant can look at the photo and tell you.

Try it:
1. Open your AI assistant — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or whichever you use.
2. Tap the + or paperclip button and choose the photo from your phone.
3. Type: "Please describe this photo in simple words. Read out any words or numbers you can see."
4. Read the answer, then ask a follow-up: "Is there a date or a phone number in it?"

This is handy for photos of signs, medicine labels, handwritten notes, or a grandchild's school notice — anything where the words matter more than the picture.

Give it a try with a photo you received recently, and tell us how it went. And if there is something you would like to learn next, suggest a topic — the next lesson comes from you.
