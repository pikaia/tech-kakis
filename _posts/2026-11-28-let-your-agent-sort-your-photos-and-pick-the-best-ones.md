**Let Your Agent Sort Your Photos — and Pick the Best Ones**

A chatbot answers one question at a time. An agent carries a job. Here is a job your assistant can carry: sorting through a pile of photos.

After a family outing or a holiday, most of us have dozens of photos — some blurry, some nearly the same, a few real gems. Instead of scrolling through them all yourself, hand the whole pile to your assistant and ask it to pick the best five to share with the family.

Try it — one sitting, using your own photos:

1. Pick a folder or album from a recent outing, with ten or more photos in it.
2. Tell your assistant: "Look through these photos and pick the five best ones to share with my family. Tell me why you picked each one."
3. Look at its five picks. Agree, disagree, or ask it to swap one out — it remembers your choices.
4. Ask it to write one short caption for each chosen photo, in your own words.

That is the agent difference: you gave it a job, it worked through your files, and it came back with a shortlist. Not just an answer — work done.

Do share how it went — did it pick the ones you would have picked? And do suggest topics for future days.
