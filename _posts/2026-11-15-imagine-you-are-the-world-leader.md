Today's idea comes from Sandra — thank you, Sandra! She asked: could we get countries to talk together about how AI should be used, instead of each one making its own rules?

Here is a fun way to try it yourself. Pretend you are the world leader who calls the meeting. Your AI assistant can play the other countries at the table.

Try it:

1. Open your AI assistant and say: "Let's pretend I am hosting a meeting of world leaders about AI. China, the United States, Singapore and the European Union are at the table."
2. Ask: "What are three simple rules all of us could agree on, so AI is used safely and fairly?"
3. Then ask: "Where would these countries disagree, and what compromise could work?"
4. Finally ask: "Write our agreement as five short sentences, in plain language anyone can understand."

You will see how an assistant can help you think through a big question step by step — and how different views can still meet in the middle.
