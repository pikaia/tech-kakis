**Your agent can keep a diary for you — and read it back**

Some things are too small to write down, and too easy to forget. "I watered the orchids." "Auntie May's birthday is next Tuesday." "The plumber is coming on Thursday."

Your assistant can hold those little notes for you — in your own words, in order — and hand them back when you ask. "What did I tell you this week?" "When is the plumber coming?" That is the difference an agent makes: it does not just answer and move on. It carries the job with you, day after day.

Nothing leaves your own words — no passwords, no account details, just the everyday things you choose to tell it.

Try it today:
1. Open your assistant and say: "Please keep a note for me."
2. Tell it three small things from today, one at a time.
3. Tomorrow, ask: "What did I tell you yesterday?" and see it read your notes back.

How did it go? Share what your assistant remembered for you, and suggest a topic for a future day.
