**Turn a Recipe into a Shopping List**

Do you have a recipe you love, but dread writing out the shopping list? Your AI assistant can do the boring part for you.

Type or paste the recipe into your assistant - or take a photo of the recipe page and ask it to read it. Then ask: "Make me a shopping list from this recipe."

Here is the useful part: ask it to sort the list by shop section - vegetables, meat, dry goods - so you walk the shop once instead of back and forth. And tell it what you already have at home, so it leaves those items off the list.

Try it tonight:

1. Pick a recipe you plan to cook this week.
2. Paste it into your AI assistant, or send a photo of the page.
3. Ask: "Make a shopping list, grouped by shop section, and leave out rice, oil and salt - I already have those."
4. Check the list against your fridge and cupboard, and correct anything it got wrong.

That last step matters. The assistant is fast, but you are the expert on your own kitchen.
