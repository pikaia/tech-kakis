**Let Your Agent Plan a Morning Out — and Report Back**

A chatbot answers one question at a time. An agent carries a whole job for you — and comes back with the answer.

Try this: pick somewhere you would like to go tomorrow morning — a park, a market, a friend's place. Then give your assistant the whole job in one go, like this:

1. Tell it where you want to go, and that you are going tomorrow morning.
2. Ask it to check tomorrow's weather and suggest the best time to go.
3. Ask it to work out how to get there by bus or MRT, and how long it takes.
4. Ask it to make a short list of what to bring.

Then sit back. Your assistant works through the steps and reports back in one answer — weather, timing, route, and list, all together. If something looks wrong, just say so and it will fix it.

That is the agent difference: you hand over the job, not just the question.

Try it tonight, and tell us tomorrow how it went — and what job you would like your agent to carry next.
