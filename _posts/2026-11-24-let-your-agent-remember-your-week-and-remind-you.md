**Let Your Agent Remember Your Week — and Remind You**

A chatbot answers a question and forgets it. An agent carries a job — and one of the simplest jobs is remembering your week for you.

Tell your assistant your regular routine once, in your own words. It keeps that note, and from then on it can remind you what is on each day, without you having to keep the whole week in your head.

Try it today:

1. Open your assistant and tell it your week's regular things, in plain words. For example: "I walk on Monday, Wednesday and Friday mornings. Market day is Saturday. I take my medicine at 8 in the morning and 8 at night."
2. Ask it: "Please keep this as my weekly routine, and each morning remind me what is on for today."
3. Tomorrow morning, ask it: "What do I have today?" See what it says.
4. When something changes, just tell it — "No walk this Friday" — and it updates the note.

That is the difference in one sentence: a chatbot answers; an agent remembers, and reminds you at the right time.

Only share what you are comfortable telling it — your plans, lists and routines. Never passwords, bank details, one-time codes or Singpass.

How did it go? Share what your assistant remembered for you, and suggest a topic you would like us to try next.
