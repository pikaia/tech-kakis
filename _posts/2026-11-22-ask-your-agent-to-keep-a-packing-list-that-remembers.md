**Ask Your Agent to Keep a Packing List That Remembers**

A chatbot can make you a packing list once. An agent can keep one for you — and make it better every trip.

Tell your assistant about the trips you take, and what you forgot last time. It will keep the list, remember what you missed, and remind you the day before you travel. That is the difference: a chatbot answers and forgets; an agent carries the job with you.

Try it tonight:

1. Tell your assistant about your last trip. For example: "I visit my daughter in Johor Bahru often, 3 days each time. Last trip I forgot my umbrella and my blood pressure medicine."
2. Ask: "Keep a packing list for my Johor trips, and remember the umbrella and medicine — I always forget those."
3. Next time you travel, ask it the day before: "Remind me what to pack for Johor."
4. After the trip, tell it what you forgot this time — the list gets better with every trip.

One small job, done in one sitting — and a list that never starts from zero again.
