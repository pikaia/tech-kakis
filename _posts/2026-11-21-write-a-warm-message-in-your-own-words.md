**Write a Warm Message in Your Own Words**

A birthday is coming up, or you want to thank someone — but the right words will not come. Your AI assistant can help you find them.

Tell it who the message is for and what you want to say. For example: "Help me write a birthday wish for my granddaughter. She just started university, and I am proud of her."

The assistant will draft something. Then make it yours: ask it to make the message shorter, warmer, or more like how you normally speak. You can even give it a small detail only you know — "she loves durians" — and ask it to work that in.

Try it tonight:

1. Think of someone you want to send a message to.
2. Tell your AI assistant who they are and why the message matters.
3. Read the draft, and ask for one change: shorter, warmer, or in simpler words.
4. Send it in your own name — it is your message, the assistant only helped with the words.
