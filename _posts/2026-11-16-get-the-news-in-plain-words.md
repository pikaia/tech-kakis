**Get the news in plain words**

The news is full of words that sound important but explain nothing — "tariffs", "fiscal policy", "geopolitical tensions". Your AI assistant can translate them into everyday language, in seconds.

You don't need to understand the jargon first. You only need to copy the sentence that puzzles you and ask.

Try it tonight:

1. Pick one news story or headline that confused you today.
2. Copy a sentence or two from it, paste it into your assistant, and ask: "Explain this to me in simple, plain words, in about five short sentences."
3. Then ask one follow-up: "What does this mean for ordinary people like me?"
4. If one word still puzzles you, ask: "What does [the word] mean, in everyday language?"
5. Try it again with a second story — it gets easier each time.

The trick is to ask for plain words, and to keep asking until it makes sense. Your assistant never gets tired of explaining, and it never judges you for asking.

How did it go? Share what you tried — and suggest a topic you'd like for a future evening.
