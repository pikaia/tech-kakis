**Check Before You Share — Ask Your AI to Vet a Video**

We all get forwarded videos that look real but aren't — a famous person saying something they never said, or an old clip dressed up as breaking news. Before you believe it or pass it on, your AI assistant can help you check.

A video is genuine only if the who, what and where all match. Tell your assistant about the video — or send it a still picture from the video — and ask it to search for the same event from trusted news sources.

Try it now:
1. Pick a video someone forwarded to you that made you raise an eyebrow.
2. Ask your assistant: "Is this video real? Who is in it, when was it first posted, and has any trusted news source reported the same event?"
3. Ask it to explain its reasons in plain words, and to show you the source it checked.
4. If it cannot find a trusted source that matches, do not forward it — and tell the sender what you found.
